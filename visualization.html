Boulder Bicycle Crash Snapshot

The data is clear: safer riding habits, outreach projects, and infrastructure projects have all combined to make riding in Boulder safer. While it can be difficult to tie these trends to specific actions, it is clear that the steps Boulder has taken with the Vision Zero Project and in their infrastructure investments show what they are working on. Boulder is becoming even more a hub for cyclists, both recreational and commuting, as riding conditions become safer. Improved riding strategies, along with safer equipment (especially in a college city), will lead to fewer collisions and fewer deaths.

Fewer collisions lead to more riding, which is better for the environment, city health, enjoyment, and traffic. Everyone benefits from cycling infrastructure, even if it is a little bit annoying at first. Drive safe, ride safe, and keep our cyclists safe!
