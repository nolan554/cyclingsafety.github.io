Our Values

Sustainability

Sustainability is another value at the core of a commuter’s life. Many cyclists do so in order to save money, be efficient, avoid traffic, and help reduce carbon emissions. Since modes of transport such as bikes, buses, and walking have all been shown to decrease carbon emissions, these values should be preserved when thinking about our transport system. We aim to protect the environment by balancing the infrastructure’s impact on the environment and weighing it against the emissions balance of the rest of Boulder.

Safety

The base of our project is safety. Protecting the lives of all who use our roads, paths, and transportation system is our highest priority. This means that there should be no traffic deaths or debilitating accidents. Pedestrians should be able to walk, drivers should be able to transit normally, and cyclists should be able to ride where they need to without injury. All movement in the city should be safe. To do this, we will promote proper infrastructure, promote safe driving/riding habits, advocate for situational awareness, and partake in safe habits ourselves.

Fairness

Fairness ensures that all road users are considered. Solutions to traffic collisions must balance the needs of drivers, pedestrians, and cyclists, preventing any group from being disproportionately disadvantaged.

Fulfillment

This means access to movement as an expression of freedom, or recreation, or of community. Transportation as a barrier to these values is unacceptable, and we will do everything we can to promote people’s safe, educated, and sustainable transportation.
